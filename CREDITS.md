# Credits

CivMod is a consolidation and continuation of three Civ-genre client mods. It is not
affiliated with or endorsed by any of their authors. All upstream work is credited below.

## CivModern
- Authors: Okx, Protonull
- Source: https://github.com/okx-code/civmodern
- Licence: GPL-3.0
- Carried over: macros (hold-left, hold-right, attack, ice road), item tooling
  (compacted item display, repair cost, uncompacted counts), chat coordinate links,
  and the configuration screen widget set.
- Not carried over: map, minimap, waypoints, radar and auto-navigation.

## CombatRadar
- Authors: Aleksey Terzi, MrJeremyFisher
- Source: https://github.com/MrJeremyFisher/CombatRadar
- Licence: Apache-2.0 (full text in `licenses/CombatRadar-Apache-2.0.txt`)
- Carried over: the radar, entity catalogue and icons, nametags, alerts, the nearby
  player list, player status reporting and the speedometer.

## SnitchMod
- Authors: Gjum
- Source: https://github.com/Gjum/snitchmod
- Licence: GPL-3.0-only
- Carried over: snitch tracking and storage, the JAList parser and auto-paginator,
  range and placement overlays, and snitch field previews.
